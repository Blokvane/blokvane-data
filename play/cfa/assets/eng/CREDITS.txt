CAR CLASH ARENA: 16-CAR ENGINE PACK: SOURCE CREDITS
=====================================================

This pack contains 160 separately edited engine cues for sixteen fictional
vehicles. The sound designs use adapted recordings; they are not recordings
of the pictured fictional models and do not establish their real specifications.

Source recordings retain their licenses below. Keep creator/title/source,
license links and modification notices for the CC BY components in the game's
credits or audio notices. The source authors do not endorse this pack or game.

Source-quality note: the downloaded inputs are Freesound's publicly available
high-quality MP3 preview encodes. The delivered 48 kHz PCM WAVs are edited
masters of those inputs, not new lossless captures of the original vehicles.
The original source titles identify recording donors only, not brands of
the fictional CAR CLASH ARENA vehicles.
