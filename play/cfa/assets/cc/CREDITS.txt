CAR CLASH ARENA: CAR COMBAT AUDIO CREDITS
=========================================

This pack combines credited engine, tire and Foley recordings with new
layering, synthesis, editing and mastering for CAR CLASH ARENA.
It is designed for use in games. The recorded components retain the source
licenses below. For CC BY 3.0 components, retain the creator/title/source,
license link and adaptation notice in your game's credits or audio notices.
The source authors do not endorse this pack or the game.

The crash cues are designed Foley assemblies, not field recordings of real
car accidents. Engine-family treatments are sound-design profiles, not
claims that the pictured fictional cars are recordings of specific makes.
