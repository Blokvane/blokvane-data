CAR FIGHTING ARENA: CREDITS
===========================

The game, its cars, arenas and weapons were made for Blokvane. Some of its sounds are made from recordings that
other people shared. Those shared under a Creative Commons Attribution license (CC BY) are listed here, as their
licenses ask. The full list of every recording used, with the CC0 (public domain) ones too, is in
assets/cc/CREDITS.txt (the combat sounds) and assets/eng/CREDITS.txt (the engine sounds).

with other sounds and evened out):
